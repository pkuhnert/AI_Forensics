# Interrogate AI

This repository houses content for the position paper titled "A Statistical-Forensic Framing for Interrogating AI/ML Systems"  

The Interrogate AI Tool that enables a statistical-forensic investigation is located here: [https://pkshinyapps.shinyapps.io/radial/](https://pkshinyapps.shinyapps.io/radial/).

To replicate the two case studies provided in the paper, we include two Excel spreadsheets that house the information related to how each AI system aligns with the Responsible Application Driven AI (RAD-AI) principles outlined in Hartman et al. (2025). An Excel template is also provided to allow users to create their own forensic investigation of an AI system.  Details of the two case studies are summarised below.

## Case Study 1: A Clinical Prediction Model for early stroke detection

This case study invokes a statistical-forensic investigation into an AI tool for stroke detection using facial images extracted from a Kaggle dataset.  The paper, since retracted, appeared in Nature Scientific Reports and was flagged in a  [Retraction Watch Article](https://retractionwatch.com/2026/05/18/kaggle-dataset-clinical-models-stroke-diabetes/).

Forensic investigation: InterrogateAI_Assessment_Stroke.xlsx


## Case Study 2: Impact of GLP-1 Receptor Agonists in Reducing Cancer Risk

Interrogate AI was applied to a health study that examined the impact of GLP-1 receptor agonists (i.e., Ozempic and other weight loss drugs like it) on cancer risk in patients who underwent treatment for obesity and diabetes.  This paper made use of the TriNetX platform, here the AI tool used to select the cohort of patients and perform the statistical analysis.  The paper outlining the methods and results appears in Levy et al. (2025).

Forensic investigation: InterrogateAI_Assessment_GLP-1.xlsx
